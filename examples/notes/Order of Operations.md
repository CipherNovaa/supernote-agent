# Order of Operations

## Rule
==Brackets, Exponents, Multiply/Divide, Add/Subtract== — multiply/divide and add/subtract each go **left to right**.

## Step-by-Step
Solve $4^2 + (15 / 3) \times 2 - 7$

1. Brackets: $15 / 3 = 5$ → $4^2 + 5 \times 2 - 7$
2. Exponents: $4^2 = 16$ → $16 + 5 \times 2 - 7$
3. Multiply: $5 \times 2 = 10$ → $16 + 10 - 7$
4. Add/subtract left to right: $16 + 10 - 7 = 19$
