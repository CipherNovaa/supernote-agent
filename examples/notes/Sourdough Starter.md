# Sourdough Starter

## Feeding
- 50 g starter, 50 g flour, 50 g water
- Feed every 24 h at room temperature
- Ready when it ==doubles within 6 hours==

## Signs something is off
- Grey liquid on top → hungry, feed sooner
- Pink or orange streaks → throw it out

> Keep a rubber band on the jar to mark the level after feeding.
